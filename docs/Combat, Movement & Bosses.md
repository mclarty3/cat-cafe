## Movement
- `[DECIDED]` (2026-09-30) Boss cats give a permanent meta-progression ability and also join the cat roster. The *source* of the ability is leaning toward the nightmare energy rather than the cat itself; see [[Cats#Boss cats]].
- `[PROPOSED]` Each **rescued boss cat grants a movement ability** that fits it, directly tying rescues to map progression (like Dead Cells' runes). Brainstormed abilities:
  - Pounce: a double jump
  - Claw grip: wall climbing
  - Zoomies: a dash
  - Squeeze: passing through tight gaps
  - Righting reflex: surviving long falls or changing direction in midair

## Weapons & loadout

- `[LEANING]` (2026-09-30) **Three layers:** one **principal weapon** that stays permanent and gets upgraded over the course of the game (the core feel, like Hollow Knight's nail); **secondary weapon(s)** found during runs for variety; and **cat traits** from the loadout on top, like Hollow Knight's charms.
- `[OPEN]` What the principal weapon actually is. Ryan wants something **"a little weapon-ier"** than a cafe tool.
- `[LEANING]` (2026-09-30) The principal weapon is **the relative's object**: something like a **pin** that the character wears as an accessory by day and that **transforms into a weapon in the dream**.
- `[LEANING]` (2026-09-30) **Sword-like rather than scythe-like.**
- `[LEANING]` (2026-09-30) **A brooch that becomes a balanced straight sword**, with slashing combos and a feel like Hollow Knight's nail. The brooch's jewel becomes the pommel or crossguard. This is a starting point, to be tuned once it can be prototyped and playtested.
- `[PROPOSED]` *(Claude suggestion)* **Upgrades show on the brooch during the day.** Each upgrade adds a stone, charm or clasp to the brooch, and the same piece appears on the sword at night. Regulars can notice and comment on it.
- `[PROPOSED]` *(Claude suggestion)* The missing cat **recognises the brooch** when you find it.
- `[PROPOSED]` *(Claude suggestion)* The brooch has always worked this way, so the **relative was going into the dream too**. That would explain why the cafe fell into disrepair. See [[Setting & Lore]].
- Also considered, but ranked below the brooch: a hatpin that becomes a rapier (too close to Hornet's needle), a hairpin that becomes a katana, and a cat-shaped lapel pin (too cute).
- `[REJECTED]` (2026-09-30) Mundane cafe tools as the main weapon (**broom, rolling pin**). Not weapon-y enough.
- `[OPEN]` What the secondary weapons are, how many you can carry, and whether they're lost when a run ends.

## Bosses
- `[DECIDED]` **Possessed cats** are major bosses. Defeating one rescues the cat.
- `[OPEN]` **Do bosses respawn** in areas you've already cleared? Ryan raised this; the two-tier model below is the likely answer.
- `[LEANING]` (2026-09-30) Two kinds of bosses:
  1. **Possessed cats are one-time story bosses.** Beating one frees the cat, which comes home and grants an ability. They **don't respawn**, since fighting the same cat again would undercut the rescue. Afterward, the area **visibly changes** as the nightmare lifts (new palette and music, maybe new rooms), leaving a permanent mark on a world that otherwise resets.
  2. **Biome guardians** are non-cat nightmare creatures that **respawn every run** and gate the way between biomes (like Dead Cells' Concierge). They give runs structure and repeatable challenge, and drop rarer ingredients.
- `[PROPOSED]` Optional **"bad dream" rematches**: a rescued cat sometimes has nightmares, which appear as harder, optional fights against an echo of its possession, offered as a bounty or cafe event ("Mochi hasn't been sleeping well..."). This adds high-end replay value (like Hollow Knight's Pantheons) without undoing a rescue.

- `[PROPOSED]` (2026-09-30) *(Ryan's idea)* **The final boss is the relative's lost cat**, taken over by the nightmare. See [[Setting & Lore#Main story]].

## Combat tone
- `[PROPOSED]` You're fighting **the thing possessing the cat**, not the cat. Hits knock shadow off it, gradually revealing the cat underneath. The final phase shifts from combat into a short **befriending moment**, tying the fight back to the cozy side.
