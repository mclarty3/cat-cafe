# Setting & Lore

Part of [[- Overview|Cat Cafe Dungeon Crawler]] · status tags: see the legend in the hub · [[Decision Log]] · [[Open Questions]]

- `[DECIDED]` The dungeon is **otherworldly and dreamlike**. It is **NOT** a physical multi-level complex under the cafe.
- `[PROPOSED]` Folklore basis: cats are creatures that move between worlds. At night they wander into this dream realm. Some get lost there, and rare breeds come from it.
- `[PROPOSED]` The dreamlike tone lets the dungeon be eerie, strange, or a little melancholy without clashing with the cozy cafe, which a gory dungeon would.
- `[PROPOSED]` Narrative frame: the player inherits a relative's rundown cafe. The relative's beloved old cat is missing somewhere in the dream realm, which creates a long-term mystery that gradually opens deeper areas (similar to Dave the Diver's Blue Hole).

## How you get into the dream
- `[DECIDED]` (2026-09-30) **Sleep/dream entry**, not a portal. You go to sleep and literally enter a dream. It fits roguelite conventions: the dream rearranges itself every night (which explains procedural layouts), and dying means waking up in bed rather than a true death. Ryan has since described exits as "waking up and returning home," which suggests leaning this way, Confirmed on 2026-09-30.
- `[REJECTED]` (2026-09-30) Entering through a **portal**.

- `[PROPOSED]` *(Claude suggestion)* The relative's **brooch** (the player's weapon, see [[Combat, Movement & Bosses]]) has always opened the dream, so the relative was going in at night too, which is why the cafe fell apart.

## Main story
- `[PROPOSED]` (2026-09-30) *(Ryan's idea)* **The relative's lost cat is the final boss.** It has been taken over by the nightmare, and the player frees it and brings it home. **The game continues after this** as a post-game.

## Player character
- `[LEANING]` (2026-10-01) A **cute young woman** who fits the cozy, dreamy style. Loosely inspired by Hat Kid (*A Hat in Time*) but not a copy: older, at roughly **4.5 to 5 heads tall** (stylized and cute, but not a child), with simple, chunky shapes that still read from the pulled-back cafe camera.
- `[LEANING]` (2026-10-01) **"Pajama Knight":** she enters the dream by falling asleep, so **in the dream she wears her sleepwear**: oversized pajamas or a nightshirt, soft socks or slippers, and a **floppy nightcap** as her signature silhouette piece (the role Hat Kid's hat plays). This gives the dream half an instantly different look from the cafe half.
- `[LEANING]` (2026-10-01) The **brooch is not on the pajamas**. She wears it by day; in the dream it *is* the sword, so it's in her hand.
- `[REJECTED]` (2026-10-01) **Cat motifs on the character** (ear-shaped buns, paw-print buttons, tail-like ribbons). There are already plenty of cats.
- Reference characters from the brainstorm: Kiki (*Kiki's Delivery Service*), Stella (*Spiritfarer*), Sophie (*Howl's Moving Castle*), Claire (*A Short Hike*), Hornet (*Hollow Knight*, for a combat-readable silhouette).
- Still open: her name and personality, her day outfit, her palette, and any customization.
