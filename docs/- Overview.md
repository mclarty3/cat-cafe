# Cat Cafe Dungeon Crawler

*Working title, placeholder.* Early concept, being designed with a friend. Seeded from a brainstorm with Claude in the web UI.

A game split between **running a cozy cat cafe by day** and **exploring a dreamlike otherworld by night** to rescue cats and gather rare ingredients.

- `[DECIDED]` Genre lineage: day business / night dungeon, in the family of **Recettear** (Ryan's first game of this kind and the original inspiration), **Moonlighter**, and **Dave the Diver**.
- `[DECIDED]` The business is a **cat cafe**: making coffee and food, handling cat-related events, and talking with customers.
- `[DECIDED]` The dungeon is where you find **rare cats** and **ingredients** for better coffee and pastries.
- `[DECIDED]` The dungeon is the **main focus**, at roughly **65% dungeon / 35% cafe** (for comparison, Dave the Diver is about 80/20).

## Design pillars

1. **Cozy cafe.** Warm, calm, jazzy, comforting. The cafe should feel like a place you want to be. Reference: **Coffee Talk**.
2. **Satisfying action in the dream dungeon.** Good movement, platforming, and combat. References: **Hollow Knight**, **Dead Cells**.
3. **Variety that keeps unfolding.** New systems, minigames, and areas keep appearing over the course of the game. Reference: **Dave the Diver** (restaurant, ocean depths, farm, merfolk village and nearby dungeon, and many small minigames such as pouring tea and grating wasabi).
4. **Cats connect everything.** Cats matter in both halves of the game, which ties the two halves together in both directions rather than the dungeon just supplying the shop.
5. **Emotional connection.** Regulars with ongoing stories, cats you rescue and care for, and bittersweet adoptions.

## Reference games

| Game | What we're taking from it |
|---|---|
| Recettear | Overall business-by-day, dungeon-by-night loop |
| Moonlighter | Shop + roguelite dungeon; voluntary return with loot (the pendant) |
| Dave the Diver | Constantly expanding systems, varied minigames, staff who add capacity |
| Coffee Talk | Cozy atmosphere, conversations over drinks, customer stories |
| Hollow Knight | Movement, combat feel, metroidvania structure |
| Dead Cells | Roguelite structure, fixed biome map with shuffled handcrafted rooms, permanent movement unlocks (runes), timed doors |
